    Meet Dr. Dezham

    More than 20 years of general and cosmetic dentistry in Sacramento.

    Your dentist

    Beauty and strength, in every tooth

    Dr. Hassan Dezham has cared for Sacramento smiles since 2004. He's a general dentist with a passion for cosmetic work.

    Patients know him for an artistic approach: crowns, veneers and fillings shaped and shaded so carefully they're hard to tell from natural teeth, and built to hold up for years.

    He runs A Miracle Smile as a solo practice, so you see the same dentist at every visit. He knows your history, remembers your goals, and never hands you off.

    Dr. Dezham earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from The University of Texas Health Science Center at San Antonio.
