Where artistry meets dentistry

A Miracle Smile by Dr. Dezham Dental Group is a Sacramento practice built around one idea: dental work should be as beautiful as it is strong.

Meet your dentist

Dr. Hassan M. Dezham, DDS

Dr. Dezham is a general dentist with a cosmetic focus who has been caring for patients for 22 years.

He's known for an artistic approach to restorative work — balancing natural-looking beauty with the strength and durability a restoration needs to last. Whether he's placing a single filling or designing a set of veneers, the goal is the same: work that looks right, feels right and holds up.

As the practice's sole dentist, Dr. Dezham personally handles your treatment, so you'll see the same familiar face at every visit.
