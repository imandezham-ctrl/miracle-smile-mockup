    Services

    Everything from a six-month cleaning to a full smile makeover, all under Dr. Dezham's care.

    01 · Preventive

    Keep teeth healthy

    The cheapest dental work is the work you never need. Regular visits catch small problems early.

    Professional cleaningsUsually every six months

    We remove the plaque and tartar that brushing and flossing miss, then polish your teeth smooth. It's the single best habit for healthy gums.

    Exams & X-raysTeeth, gums and bite

    Dr. Dezham checks every tooth, your gums and your bite, with X-rays as needed to see what the eye can't, like decay between teeth or below the gumline.

    Oral cancer screeningsPart of every checkup

    A quick, painless check of your mouth, tongue and throat for early warning signs. Caught early, oral cancer is far easier to treat.

    Sealants for childrenCavity protection for kids

    A thin protective coating painted onto the chewing surfaces of back teeth, where most children's cavities start. Quick, painless and no drilling.

    Gum disease therapyPeriodontal treatment

    Bleeding, puffy or receding gums are signs of gum disease. Treatment cleans below the gumline to stop the infection and protect the bone that holds your teeth.

    02 · Restorative

    Repair and restore

    Fix decay and damage with restorations shaped and shaded to blend in with your own teeth.

    FillingsTooth-colored

    Cavities are cleaned out and filled with a natural-looking material that bonds to the tooth, so the repair is strong and hard to spot.

    BondingChips, gaps and worn edges

    A tooth-colored resin is shaped right onto the tooth to fix chips, close small gaps or cover stains, often in a single visit.

    Crowns & bridgeworkRebuild a tooth, or replace one

    A crown caps a cracked, worn or heavily filled tooth to make it strong again. A bridge anchors a replacement tooth to the teeth on either side of a gap.

    Root canal treatmentSave an infected tooth

    When infection reaches the nerve inside a tooth, a root canal removes it, seals the tooth and stops the pain, so you keep your natural tooth.

    Tooth extractionsWhen a tooth can't be saved

    If a tooth is too damaged or infected to repair, we remove it gently and talk you through options to replace it, like an implant or bridge.

    03 · Implants

    Replace a missing tooth

    The closest thing to growing a new tooth.

    Dental implantsA permanent replacement

    An implant is a small post placed in the jawbone that acts like a tooth root. Once it heals, it's topped with a crown that looks, feels and chews like a natural tooth. Implants don't rely on neighboring teeth and help keep the jawbone healthy.

    Every case starts with an exam and X-rays to make sure an implant is the right fit for you.

    04 · Cosmetic

    A smile you love

    Dr. Dezham's focus. Beautiful results that still look like you.

    Porcelain veneersReshape and brighten front teeth

    Thin porcelain shells bonded to the front of your teeth to change their shape, size or color. They hide chips, gaps, stains and uneven edges, and resist staining.

    Teeth whiteningProfessional brightening

    Lift years of coffee, tea and wine stains with professional whitening that's stronger than drugstore kits and supervised for comfort.

    Cosmetic bondingSmall changes, one visit

    For a chipped edge or a small gap, bonding can change your smile in a single appointment, without removing tooth structure.

    05 · Orthodontics

    Straighter teeth

    Three ways to straighten teeth, so the choice is about what works for you.

    Clear alignersNearly invisible, removable

    A series of custom clear trays that move your teeth a little at a time. Take them out to eat and brush. Popular with adults and teens.

    Traditional bracesReliable for complex cases

    Brackets and wires remain the most versatile way to correct crowding, gaps and bite problems.

    Lingual bracesHidden behind your teeth

    Braces bonded to the back of your teeth, so no one sees them when you smile.

    06 · Jaw pain & emergencies

    Out of pain, fast

    For the problems that can't wait for your next checkup.

    Emergency dental careCall (916) 441-3311

    Severe toothache, a broken or knocked-out tooth, a lost crown or swelling: call right away and we'll do our best to see you quickly.

    Call the office

    TMJ / TMD treatmentJaw pain, clicking and clenching

    Problems with the jaw joint can cause soreness, clicking, headaches and worn teeth from grinding. An exam finds the cause, and Dr. Dezham will walk you through the treatment options.
