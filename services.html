Cosmetic dentistry

Porcelain Veneers

Ultra-thin porcelain shells are bonded to the front of your teeth to correct chips, stains, gaps or uneven shapes. Each veneer is shaded and contoured to look natural next to your other teeth.

  • Stain-resistant
  • Custom shaded
  • Smile makeovers

Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening lifts stains from coffee, tea, wine and time more effectively than over-the-counter products, with guidance on keeping your results bright.

  • Brighter smile
  • Professionally supervised

Bonding

Tooth-colored composite resin is sculpted directly onto a tooth to repair small chips, close minor gaps or improve its shape — often in a single visit.

  • Conservative
  • Often one visit

Orthodontics

Straighter teeth are easier to clean and more comfortable to bite with. We offer several ways to get there, so you can choose the option that fits your lifestyle.

  • Clear aligners
  • Traditional braces
  • Lingual braces

Restorative dentistry

Dental Implants

An implant replaces the root of a missing tooth and supports a lifelike restoration on top. It's a long-term option that helps preserve the jawbone and the alignment of neighboring teeth.

  • Replaces missing teeth
  • Natural look & feel

Crowns & Bridgework

Crowns cap and protect teeth weakened by decay, cracks or large fillings. Bridges span the gap left by one or more missing teeth using neighboring teeth for support.

  • Strength + beauty
  • Custom crafted

Root Canal Treatment

When the inner pulp of a tooth becomes infected or inflamed, a root canal removes the damaged tissue, seals the tooth and relieves pain — helping you keep your natural tooth.

  • Relieves pain
  • Saves the tooth

Fillings

Cavities are cleaned out and restored with a filling to stop decay from spreading and bring the tooth back to full function.

  • Stops decay
  • Restores function

Tooth Extractions

When a tooth is too damaged to save or is causing crowding, a gentle extraction may be the healthiest choice. We'll walk you through replacement options afterward.

  • Gentle approach
  • Replacement planning

Preventive & general care

Professional Teeth Cleanings

Regular cleanings remove plaque and tartar that brushing can't reach, and give us the chance to catch small issues before they become bigger ones.

  • Plaque & tartar removal
  • Routine checkups

Sealants for Children

A thin protective coating painted onto the chewing surfaces of back teeth helps keep food and bacteria out of deep grooves where cavities often start.

  • Kids' prevention
  • Quick & painless

Periodontal (Gum) Disease Therapy

Bleeding, swollen or receding gums can be signs of gum disease. Treatment focuses on removing bacteria below the gumline and helping your gums heal.

  • Healthier gums
  • Deep cleaning

Oral Cancer Screenings

A quick visual and physical check of the mouth, tongue and throat for unusual changes. Early detection makes a real difference.

  • Quick exam
  • Early detection

TMJ / TMD Treatment

Jaw pain, clicking, headaches or teeth grinding can be linked to the temporomandibular joint. We'll evaluate your symptoms and discuss options to help you find relief.

  • Jaw pain
  • Clicking & grinding

Emergency Dental Care

Severe toothache, a knocked-out or broken tooth, a lost crown or facial swelling all need prompt attention. Call the office as soon as possible so we can help.

  • Toothaches
  • Broken teeth
  • Lost crowns

In pain right now? Call (916) 441-3311 during office hours. If you have trouble breathing or swallowing, or uncontrolled bleeding, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.
