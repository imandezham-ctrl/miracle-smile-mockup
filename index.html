    FAQ

    Common questions

    Don't see yours? Call us and we'll answer it in plain English.

    Do you take my dental insurance?

    Call us with your insurance provider and plan name and we'll check your benefits before your visit, so you know what's covered before any treatment starts.

    What should I bring to my first visit?

    A photo ID, your insurance card, and a list of any medications you take. If you have recent X-rays from another dentist, let us know and we can request them.

    How often should I get a cleaning?

    Most people do well with a cleaning and checkup every six months. If you're being treated for gum disease, Dr. Dezham may recommend coming in more often.

    Do you see children?

    Yes. We see patients of all ages, and we offer sealants to help protect children's back teeth from cavities.

    What counts as a dental emergency?

    Severe tooth pain, a cracked, broken or knocked-out tooth, a lost filling or crown, or swelling in your gums or face. Call us as soon as you can. If a tooth is knocked out, keep it moist (in milk works) and call right away.

    Can you help with jaw pain or teeth grinding?

    Yes. Dr. Dezham treats TMJ and TMD, the jaw-joint problems behind clicking, soreness, headaches and clenching. The first step is an exam to find what's causing it.

    Are clear aligners right for me?

    Clear aligners work well for many adults and teens. Some cases do better with traditional or lingual braces, which sit behind the teeth. We offer all three, so the recommendation is about what works best for you.

    Where do I park?

    We're at 3009 K Street, Suite 255. Call the front desk and we'll tell you the easiest place to park.
